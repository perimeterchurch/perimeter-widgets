# staff-directory

A responsive grid of **staff photo cards**. Each card shows the person's
headshot with their name and job title over it, and links to their
staff-contact form. A search box and a department dropdown sit above the grid.

This is the modern rebuild of the `reactwidgets` **Staff** widget. Two things
changed underneath it: the data now comes from Ministry Platform's `HR_Personnel`
/ `HR_Positions` tables rather than the retired `api_custom_Staff_Widget` stored
proc, and cards link by **Contact GUID** rather than the old
`?eid=<Employee ID>`.

Staff are sorted alphabetically by last name and limited to people published to
the website in Ministry Platform whose record has not ended.

## Embed

```html
<script src="https://widgets.perimeter.org/loader.js" async></script>
<div data-perimeter-widget="staff-directory"></div>
```

Pin it to one department on a per-ministry staff page — this also hides the
department dropdown, so a visitor cannot widen past the page they are on:

```html
<div data-perimeter-widget="staff-directory" data-ministry-ids="14"></div>
```

Show a hand-picked roster (a leadership page, for instance) with the heading and
search removed:

```html
<div
  data-perimeter-widget="staff-directory"
  data-contact-ids="583177,598448,606974"
  data-title=""
  data-intro=""
  data-show-search=""
></div>
```

## Filters

A keyword search and a **department** dropdown. Search matches a person's name
and every one of their position titles and departments, so someone searching a
person's second role still finds them.

"Department" is the reader-facing name for MP's `Ministries`, which replaced the
old `Departments` table. The dropdown options are **data-driven** — they come
from `/api/staff-directory/facets`, narrowed to departments at least one visible
staff member actually belongs to, so the list never offers an empty one.

## Multiple positions

A person can hold more than one position, and the card shows every title,
joined with a middle dot. The legacy widget had a hardcoded special case for the
one employee who spanned two ministries; that is now just what the data says.

Titles are ordered longest-held first, so the primary role reads first.

## Photos

Headshots come from Ministry Platform. Roughly two in five staff have none on
file, and those cards render the person's initials on a plain tile — no extra
request. Set `data-default-photo-url` to use a shared placeholder image instead.

Photos are lazy-loaded, which matters here: MP serves each headshot at full
resolution (around 1 MB), so a grid of 350 people would be very heavy if every
image loaded up front.

## Not included

The legacy widget's **Executive Leadership Team** and **Elder Ministry Team**
sections are not here. Nothing in the new HR tables identifies either group, and
ruling elders are not staff, so they have no personnel record at all. The
legacy `data-elt` / `data-emt` attributes have no equivalent.

`data-params`, which passed raw arguments to the stored proc, is replaced by the
`data-ministry-ids`, `data-personnel-type-ids`, and `data-contact-ids` options.

## Config

The widget reads its config from `data-*` attributes on the placeholder div. See
`src/types.ts` for the zod schema — the source of truth for available options and
defaults.
