Bypasses the loader and embeds /local/prayer-wall/index.js directly. Build first: pnpm --filter @perimeter/widget-prayer-wall build. data-api-url points at a local perimeter-api on :5500 — run pnpm dev there. The host page ships perimeter.org's real 19px type plus hostile global CSS; inside the widget the request cards must stay a plain list (no bullets, no 2rem row gaps), the buttons must stay brand blue rather than lime, and — once the form is open — the inputs must not be hotpink, the privacy radios must not be full-width, and the Privacy/Notify fieldsets must have no red dashed border.
