Bypasses the loader and embeds /local/my-missions/index.js directly. Build first: pnpm --filter @perimeter/widget-my-missions build. This is an authenticated widget, so it renders a sign-in prompt until an MP token is in localStorage — and its endpoints (GET /api/missions/my-trips, PUT …/{pledgeId}/letter) do not exist in perimeter-api yet, so until they do the states reachable here are the sign-in gate and the error state. data-api-url points at a local perimeter-api on :5500 — run pnpm dev there.

What must hold once trips render: the trip rows and Leaders strip stay a plain list (no square bullets, no 2rem row gaps); the donations table keeps its own thin border and full width rather than a red dashed 50%-wide box with hotpink centred-right cells; every button stays brand blue with rounded corners rather than square lime; the letter editor's contenteditable stays white-on-token with the widget's own font and its natural height, not yellow cursive at 40rem; bold text inside the letter and the description stays the widget's foreground colour, not red; and links stay brand blue without a strikethrough. Headings must not be uppercased.
